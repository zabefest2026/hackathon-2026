


## ZABEFEST – HACKATHON’26
# Hackathon Guidelines

# What to Bring
Participants are expected to bring the following items:
- Personal laptops with chargers.
- Accessories such as a mouse, headphones, or any other peripherals required for
development.
- Ensure your laptop is fully charged before arriving.

# Technology Stack – Open Platform
This hackathon encourages creativity and technical flexibility.
- No restrictions are imposed on web or mobile development stacks.
- Participants may utilize any frameworks, programming languages, or tools of
their choice.
- The primary focus should be on developing a clear and practical solution to the
given problem.


#How to Prepare
To ensure a productive hackathon experience, the following preparations are advised:
The official theme will be announced five days before the hackathon
(i.e., 4 June 2026), so participants should plan their approach accordingly while
focusing on effective time management across brainstorming, development,
testing, and presentation—since problem-solving clarity, impact, and innovation
will be the main judging criteria.

# Project Management & Code Sharing
- All updates, including the problem statement, will be communicated via
GitHub.
- Each team is required to create a GitHub repository and grant access to the
repository to the Zab E Fest email: zabefest@szabist.pk
- All final code must be uploaded to GitHub by the conclusion of the hackathon.
- Physical presence on the final day is mandatory for demonstrations and
submissions.

# No Plagiarism Allowed
- Copying code or project structures from other participants or public sources is
strictly prohibited.
- All submissions must be original and developed entirely during the event.
- Any team found violating this policy will be disqualified immediately.

#Rules of Conduct
We  are  committed  to  fostering  a  safe,  inclusive,  and  respectful  environment.  All
participants are expected to adhere to the following:
- Show respect toward peers, mentors, and organizers.
- Engage in kind collaboration — healthy competition is welcome.
- Maintain a professional demeanor during presentations and interactions.
- Harassment, discrimination, or offensive behavior will not be tolerated.
- Refrain from disturbing other teams during working sessions.

Failure to comply with these rules may result in disqualification or removal from the
event.

# Evaluation Date
All team projects will be evaluated on June 9, 2026. Teams must ensure that:
- Final code is pushed to GitHub.
- All members are physically present for judging and demonstration.

Late submissions and remote participation will not be permitted.

# Final Checklist
- Laptop, charger, and accessories packed.
- GitHub account ready for use.
- Team members aligned and briefed.
- Time management strategy discussed.
- Mentally prepared to think creatively and solve problems.

We look forward to witnessing your innovative solutions. Prepare to demonstrate your
skills, tackle real-world challenges, and grow through collaboration and creativity.
If you have any questions before the event, please reach out to the organizing team.


## GOOD LUCK!
